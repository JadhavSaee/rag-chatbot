Q: What are the consultation fees? How much does each doctor charge?
A: General Physician: Rs 500. Dermatologist: Rs 800. Pediatrician: Rs 600.

Q: How can I book an appointment? Are walk-ins accepted?
A: Appointments can be booked by phone or at the reception. Walk-ins are accepted, but appointments get priority.

Q: What is the cancellation policy?
A: Please cancel at least 4 hours before your appointment. There is no cancellation fee.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept cash, UPI and all major debit and credit cards.

Q: How long do lab reports take?
A: Lab reports are ready within 24 hours and can be collected at the reception.

Q: Do you accept insurance?
A: We accept cashless claims for selected insurers. Please call the reception to confirm.
